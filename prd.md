# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Overview
**Product Name**: CARPAIGN
**Core Mission**: "Transforming Automotive Inventory into Content, and Content into Sales."
**Target Audience**: Automotive dealerships, car content creators, and buyers seeking high-quality automotive content and purchasing options.

## Core Features (MVP)

### 1. Dashboard
- Overview of key metrics (views, conversions, active campaigns).
- High-tech, dark mode analytics charts (using the Performance Deep Indigo & Liquid Luxury Gold accents).

### 2. Creator Marketplace
- A portal connecting automotive dealerships with content creators.
- Creators can browse available inventory and pitch campaigns.

### 3. Vehicle Inventory
- Detailed listing of automotive inventory.
- High-quality image galleries and specs.
- Integrated directly with the content creation pipeline.

### 4. Campaign Analytics
- Real-time tracking of content performance across platforms.
- ROI measurement for dealerships.

### 5. Money / Wallet (Akonay Moneys)
- Financial dashboard for creators to track earnings and for dealerships to manage campaign budgets.
